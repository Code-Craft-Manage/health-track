# Photorealistic measurement-image prompts

Ten ready-to-run prompts for generating your own photorealistic measurement
images — one per technique. Because **you** generate them, the output is yours
with no royalties, and the prompts describe the style generically (they don't
reference any existing app).

Each prompt is self-contained: **paste it straight into your image generator.**
Tips for keeping the 10 consistent, and tool-specific syntax, are at the bottom.

---

## The consistent "look" (baked into every prompt)

- **Model:** a fit athletic woman in her late twenties, toned physique,
  shoulder-length brown hair in a neat bob, light-to-medium skin, calm neutral
  expression, wearing a fitted charcoal-grey sports bra and fitted shorts (snug activewear keeps each landmark visible while staying within ChatGPT's content rules).
- **Setting:** clean minimalist photo studio, soft neutral grey background, soft
  diffused lighting, gentle shadows.
- **Camera:** photorealistic, professional fitness photography, 85mm lens, sharp
  focus, front-facing, full body centered, vertical 4:5 portrait.
- **Always:** the flexible measuring tape at the target spot is the crisp, clear
  focal point, lying flat and snug.

*(To make any card male, swap the Model line for: "a fit athletic man in his
late twenties, lean muscular build, short dark hair, bare torso, wearing grey
athletic shorts." This matches how chest is often shown for men.)*

## Getting a consistent set (important)

The 10 should look like one shoot. To do that:

1. **Keep the Model + Setting + Camera wording identical** across all prompts
   (already done above — only the pose/landmark changes).
2. **Reuse the same starting point** so the same face/body recurs — in ChatGPT,
   just keep generating in one chat; in other tools, fix the seed.
3. **Generate the NECK image first**, then reuse it as the reference the other
   nine match.

## Recommended: generate them in one chat-based tool

A conversational image generator (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) is the easiest place to
keep one model across all ten, because you can keep refining in the same thread:

1. Start **one new chat**. Paste **prompt #1 (NECK)** and add: *"Photorealistic,
   vertical 4:5 portrait."*
2. When you like it, continue in the **same chat**: *"Same woman, same outfit,
   same studio and lighting — now [paste prompt #2]."* Repeat through #10.
   Staying in one chat is what keeps the face and body consistent.
3. If it ever drifts, say *"keep her identical to the first image."*
4. Generate **one at a time**; regenerate any you don't like (*"redo — make the
   tape flatter and the pose more front-on"*).

**No rush:** if your tool caps how many images you can make per day, just do a
few a day over a few days.

**If it refuses** (it can be cautious about skin): keep the clothing as "fitted
sports bra and shorts / activewear, fitness instructional photo." Avoid the words
"underwear," "briefs," "bikini," "lingerie," or "bare" — activewear still shows
every landmark clearly.

## Other tools you can use

- **Google AI Studio** (aistudio.google.com) or the **Gemini app** — free with a
  Google account, uses Imagen; very photorealistic and good at consistency.
- **Leonardo.ai** — free daily credits, realistic Phoenix/Flux models, fairly
  lenient for fitness imagery.
- **Microsoft Copilot / Bing Image Creator** — free (DALL·E 3); easy, but stricter
  about skin, so lean hard on the activewear wording.
- **A free FLUX space on Hugging Face** (search "FLUX.1 dev") — very realistic and
  lenient; downside: hardest to keep the *same* face across images.

For any of these, make the **neck** image first and keep its settings, then reuse
the same model wording for the rest. *(Midjourney/Stable Diffusion notes live in
git history if you ever subscribe — not needed for the free path.)*
